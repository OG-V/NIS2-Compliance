# ADR 0002: Extract from CIR 2024/2690, trace to NIS2 Art. 21

**Status:** accepted

**Context:** NIS2 Art. 21(2) is too abstract to yield checkable rules. CIR (EU) 2024/2690
sets out concrete technical requirements for Art. 21(2), but only for certain digital
infrastructure and ICT service entities.

**Decision:** Extract requirements from the 2024/2690 Annex and record the NIS2 article
each one implements. The demo target is framed as an MSP, which is in scope of 2024/2690.

**Consequences:** The results are legally meaningful for the demo entity. For entities
outside 2024/2690, the catalog is best-practice guidance rather than binding law, and the
report has to say so.
