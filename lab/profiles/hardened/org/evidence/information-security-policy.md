# NordMSP — Information Security Policy

Version 4.0 · Owner: CISO · Approved by: Management board, 2026-06-12 · Next review: 2027-06

## 1. Purpose and scope
Protects the network and information systems NordMSP uses to deliver managed services,
and the customer data processed in them.

## 2. Risk management
Information security risks are assessed yearly and after significant changes, using the
method in the risk management procedure. Risk owners are named per service.

## 3. Principles
Least privilege; multi-factor authentication for all staff; encryption in transit and at
rest; central logging; tested backups; patching by severity.

## 4. Review
The policy is reviewed by management at least annually and after significant incidents or
changes to operations or risks. Review results are minuted.
