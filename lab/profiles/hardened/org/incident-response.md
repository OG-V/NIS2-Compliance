# NordMSP — Incident Response Plan

Version 2.1 · Owner: CISO · Approved by: Management board · Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## 1. Scope
All network and information systems used to deliver NordMSP's managed services.

## 2. Roles
- Incident manager: on-call security engineer
- Decision authority for external notification: CISO, deputy CEO

## 3. Detection and classification
Alerts from central logging and customer reports are triaged within 1 hour and classified
using the significance criteria in Article 23(3) of Directive (EU) 2022/2555 and
Implementing Regulation (EU) 2024/2690.

## 4. Reporting to the CSIRT (NIS2 Article 23)
For a significant incident:
- **Early warning** to the national CSIRT **within 24 hours** of becoming aware.
- **Incident notification within 72 hours** of becoming aware, with an initial assessment.
- **Intermediate report** on request of the CSIRT.
- **Final report within one month** after the incident notification.

Affected service recipients are informed without undue delay.

## 5. Contacts
- National CSIRT: NSM NorCERT (contact details maintained in the on-call runbook)
- Internal escalation: on-call rota in the paging system

## 6. Post-incident review
Every significant incident gets a documented root-cause analysis and lessons-learned review.
